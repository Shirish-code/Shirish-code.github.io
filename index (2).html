06 Principles

How I think about building systems.

01

Infrastructure as code, always

If it's not in version control, it doesn't exist. Manual cloud changes are technical debt the moment they happen. Every production resource should be reproducible from Git.

02

Automate what you do twice

Repetitive operational tasks compound into hours lost and errors made. A small Python script today saves a midnight incident next month. Toil is a real cost, not a hidden one.

03

Documentation is engineering

Undocumented architecture exists only in the heads of the people who built it. READMEs, runbooks, and architecture diagrams are first-class deliverables — written alongside the code, not after.

04

Clarity beats cleverness

The best infrastructure is the one the next engineer can reason about during an incident. Boring patterns that work at 3am beat elegant solutions that need explaining.
